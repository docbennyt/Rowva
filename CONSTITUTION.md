# Rowva Mini Offline – Constitution

1. Declared totals override heuristics.
2. Itemized sums are informative, not authoritative.
3. Cash in hand is factual, never inferred.
4. ZIMRA shared expense is excluded from totals.
5. Offline mode must be deterministic.
6. No probabilistic logic may affect final totals.
7. Parsers may suggest; reconcilers decide.
